When drafting thesis text (given 2026-11-06):
1. Use only the user's materials (repo report/PROTOCOL, papers in C:\Users\kirit\Downloads\papers) and literature; cite as （作者，年份）.
2. A claim that needs literature not in the materials → 【此處需補文獻：需要的論點】.
3. Places needing the user's research data or results → 【待填】; never fill in numbers myself.
4. Organise paragraphs by argument, not "A 認為、B 認為" lists.

**Why:** the user controls what goes into the thesis and wants no invented citations or numbers.
